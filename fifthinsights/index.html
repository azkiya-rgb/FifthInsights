Questions we get before the first call.

What is FifthInsights?

A software consultancy and product engineering studio. We build web apps, mobile apps, APIs, AI systems and multi-sided platforms for clients, and products of our own.

Who do you work with?

Anyone who needs software built or judged well, from founders shipping a first product to businesses running logistics, retail POS or restaurant ordering platforms.

What technologies do you use?

React, Next.js and React Native on the front end; Node and Postgres on the back end; cloud infrastructure; and LLM integration, agents and evals for AI work. The stack is chosen per project.

How does a project work?

Five stops: Discover, Define, Build, Launch and Evolve, with weekly increments to an environment you own.

How do I contact you?

Email hi@fifthinsights.com with what you're building and what's in the way, or find us on LinkedIn.
